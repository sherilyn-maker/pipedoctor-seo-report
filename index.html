🔧 What Every Underperforming Page Is Missing
These 8 problems apply to virtually every one of the 155+ underperforming pages
❌ #1: Template Thin Content

Every service × city page is a template with only the city name swapped. Google detects this and refuses to rank template pages above position 20–60. Each page needs 600–1,000 words of content that could only apply to that specific city: local neighborhoods, pipe age context, local code requirements, named examples.

❌ #2: No Schema Markup

Zero FAQPage schema, no Service schema on individual service pages. Implement on every page: Service schema (serviceType, areaServed, provider, priceRange) + FAQPage schema with 4–6 questions. Required for AI Overview appearances and rich snippets.

❌ #3: Duplicate Competing Pages

Multiple pages target identical queries: /sewer-camera-inspection/ vs /sewer-video-inspection/, /trenchless-sewer-repair/ vs /trenchless-pipe-repair/. These cannibalize each other. Consolidate via 301 redirect OR give each a clearly different angle with distinct content.

⚠️ #4: Weak Title Tags

All titles follow "[Service] [City] VA" with no differentiator. Required format: "[Service] [City] VA | [Value Prop] | The Pipe Doctor Plumbing". Example: "Sewer Camera Inspection Fairfax VA | Same-Day Video | The Pipe Doctor" — adds CTR differentiation Google uses for ranking.

⚠️ #5: No Cost Transparency

High-intent users want to know cost before calling. Pages with cost sections rank significantly higher. Add to every service page: "[Service] in [City] typically costs $X–$X depending on [factors]" even as an estimate range. This is the single most missing element for commercial-intent keyword rankings.

⚠️ #6: No Internal Linking Structure

Pages are completely siloed. Required: Hub page → all service sub-pages → blog posts → back to hub. Every new internal link is a ranking signal. The sewer camera page needs links from 5+ related pages to rank page 1. Currently most pages have zero cross-links.

💡 #7: No E-E-A-T Signals

Service pages have no author attribution, no actual photos from that city, no testimonials from that specific city's customers. Add per page: 1 photo of real work in that area, 1–2 review quotes from customers in that city, name of technician who covers that service area, link to relevant license/certification.

💡 #8: No FAQ Sections

Every service page needs 4–6 questions targeting: "How much does [service] cost in [city]?", "How long does [service] take?", "Do I need [service] or just [alternative]?", "Is [service] covered by insurance?" — these map directly to People Also Ask boxes and AI Overview appearances.
